1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how InboxPacer ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the InboxPacer Chrome extension ("the Extension"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being fully transparent about our practices.

By installing and using InboxPacer, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

InboxPacer operates on a strict zero data storage principle. Your emails, passwords, and personal information are never stored anywhere.

2. Information We Access and Process

2.1 Email Content (Temporary Processing Only)

When you click the InboxPacer button, the Extension reads the following from the open email:

  • Sender name and email address
  • Email subject line
  • Email body text (latest message only — quoted history is stripped)
  • Recipient type where technically available

This content is sent securely to our backend server solely to generate an AI reply. It is:

  • Never stored in any database, log file, or server storage
  • Immediately discarded after the AI reply is generated
  • Never used for any purpose other than generating your reply
  • Never associated with your identity or email account

2.2 Recipient Role Detection (To / CC / BCC)

InboxPacer attempts to detect whether you are a direct (To), CC, or BCC recipient:

  • Direct (To) — detected reliably on all supported platforms
  • CC — detected where email header data is accessible in the browser
  • BCC — cannot be detected by design
Why BCC cannot be detected: When an email is delivered, the mail server removes BCC information before delivery to protect the privacy of BCC recipients. This is a fundamental email protocol limitation that applies to all email tools worldwide — not specific to InboxPacer.

2.3 Usage Counter (Local Storage Only)

A simple reply counter is stored in your browser's local storage to manage your daily usage limit. This data:

  • Is stored only on your device — never on our servers
  • Contains only a numeric count and reset timestamp
  • Contains no personal information

2.4 Feedback (Optional)

If you choose to submit feedback, we collect your star rating, optional message, and submission date only. No name or email address is collected.

3. How We Use Information

  • Email content — to generate an AI reply using Anthropic Claude AI
  • Recipient role — to display an appropriate badge in the extension
  • Usage counter — to manage your daily free reply limit locally
  • Feedback — to improve InboxPacer features and fix issues

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other commercial purpose.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

4.1 Anthropic Claude AI

Email content is transmitted securely to Anthropic's Claude AI API solely to generate reply suggestions. Please review Anthropic's Privacy Policy for details on their data handling.

4.2 No Other Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your information with any other third parties. InboxPacer does not use advertising networks, analytics services, tracking pixels, or any other data collection services.

5. Email Platform Access

InboxPacer works inside Gmail, Yahoo Mail (all regions), Outlook Personal, Office 365, and enterprise webmail. The Extension accesses email content only when you actively click the InboxPacer button. It does not:

  • Read emails in the background
  • Scan your inbox automatically
  • Access emails you have not opened
  • Modify your email settings or account
  • Store any email content after generating a reply

6. Data Security

  • All API communications use HTTPS (TLS) encryption
  • Backend uses industry-standard security headers
  • Rate limiting protects against unauthorized access
  • No personal data or email content is ever written to disk or database
  • Session data exists only in memory and is cleared after each request

7. Children's Privacy

InboxPacer is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used InboxPacer, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Since InboxPacer does not store personal data on our servers, there is no data held by us to access or delete. Your choices include:

  • Uninstall InboxPacer at any time from Chrome
  • Clear your local usage counter by clearing browser local storage
  • Decline to submit feedback — entirely optional
  • Contact us with any privacy questions

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as we add new features. When we make material changes, we will update the version number and date at the top. Continued use of InboxPacer after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
